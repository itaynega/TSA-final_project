## 0. What five days means

The brief's floor is fixed: a reconstruction, a baseline, two metrics, a temporal protocol, an improvement
on the identical split, a 7-section PDF, a README, the dataset. Five days does not shrink the floor — it
removes what sits above it.

**We build to the floor and spend the surplus on rigour, not scope.** One benchmark, one baseline, one
improvement. What we do *not* cut: pre-registration, the leakage audit, the fact sheet. Those cost hours,
not days, and they are where the marks are.

**Report sections F1 and F2 are written on Day 1.** "Original architecture" and "paper results" depend only
on the paper, not on any experiment. Writing them while the environment installs is the single most
valuable compression available.

## 4. Deliberately not doing

Named so they are choices, not oversights, and so nobody adds them back at 2am on Day 5:

- One benchmark dataset. One baseline. One improvement.
- No hyperparameter sweep of our own — we use the paper's, and say so.
- No second improvement, however good the first one's results look.
- **No unregistered late experiment.** §3.1 exists precisely to prevent these, and a grader can smell them.
