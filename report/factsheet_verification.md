### 1a. The CRLF finding — one cause, 30 rows

`.gitattributes` line 1 is `* text=auto`, so git stores LF and materialises **CRLF
on Windows**. Every digest below was taken from a Windows working tree and matches
that file's CRLF form exactly, while the committed content — and every checkout on
macOS or Linux — is LF.

**These are not 30 separate errors. They are one systematic issue, and its effect is
that no digest in the sheet can be verified by anyone except on a Windows checkout.**
For a document whose stated purpose is that every value carries the digest of the
file it came from, that is worse than carrying no digest: a grader or teammate who
checks one will find it wrong and cannot tell a line-ending artefact from tampering.

A platform-independent digest of the same content is `git show HEAD:<path> | sha256sum`,
or hashing after normalising CRLF to LF. Not changed here — this tool reports only.
